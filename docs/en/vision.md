# Vision and principles

[English](vision.md) · [Português](../pt-BR/visao.md)

## Mission

Help people discover relevant communities, build individual connections, and participate in real-time conversations and activities under one social identity.

## Product pillars

- **Discover:** recommended public communities, interest-based search, and authorized text-channel/voice-room activity from joined communities — **no global post feed**.
- **Messaging:** private conversations between two people, not conventional group chats; photos, video, audio, documents and links remain in messages.
- **Communities:** organized public/private spaces with text channels, media sharing and live voice rooms.

These are connected journeys, not products sharing only a login. The November 2, 2026 direction is detailed in [Discover](discover.md); the legacy global feed has already been retired from the beta and its backend.

## Why start with gamers?

MMORPG communities coordinate strategies and events, chat privately, and gather in voice rooms — often across multiple apps. This makes them a useful **initial validation segment**, not a limit on who can use Uorqui.

## Product principles

1. **Participation before passive consumption.** Focus on finding people and communities and useful interaction, not infinite scrolling.
2. **Privacy by design.** Access rules apply across search, activity, media previews and notifications.
3. **No traditional messaging groups.** Collective conversations live in channels inside communities.
4. **Familiar behavior, distinct identity.** Borrow useful interaction patterns without borrowing competitor feature names.
5. **Accessible by default.** Mobile-first, desktop-capable, keyboard and assistive technology support.
6. **Operational sustainability.** Voice and media require capacity controls and cost monitoring.
7. **Open by design.** Document decisions and welcome independent contributions.

## A hypothesis, not a claim

The integrated journey **discover a community → join a channel/room → build one-to-one connections** may prove valuable. Test it with real communities before scaling.

## Explicit non-goals for the first release

Global public post feeds, infinite short-video browsing, full parity with Discord or WhatsApp, traditional group chats, large-scale livestreaming, complex AI ranking, creator marketplaces, federation and claims of end-to-end encryption before reviewed implementation.
