# Vision and principles

[English](vision.md) · [Português](../pt-BR/visao.md)

## Mission

Make it easier for people to discover interesting content, connect one-to-one, and participate in meaningful communities from the same social identity.

## Product pillars

- **Messaging:** conversations between two people, not conventional group chats.
- **Discovery:** a flexible, continuously scrolling mixed-media feed, plus a dedicated video-only immersive experience.
- **Communities:** organized public/private spaces with text channels and live voice rooms.

These are connected journeys, not separate products that happen to share a login.

## Why start with gamers?

MMORPG communities already share clips, coordinate events, chat privately, and gather in voice rooms — often across multiple apps. This makes them a useful **initial validation segment**, not a limit on who can use Uorqui.

## Product principles

1. **People before engagement metrics.** Optimize for useful interactions and retention, not compulsive passive viewing.
2. **Privacy by design.** Community visibility and membership rules apply across feed, search, media previews, and notifications.
3. **No traditional messaging groups.** Collective conversations live in channels inside communities.
4. **Familiar behavior, distinct identity.** Borrow interaction patterns only where useful; do not adopt competitor feature names as our branding.
5. **Accessible by default.** Mobile-first, desktop-capable, keyboard and assistive technology support.
6. **Operationally sustainable.** Voice and video require capacity controls and cost monitoring.
7. **Open by design.** Document decisions and welcome independent contributions.

## A hypothesis, not a claim

The integrated journey **content → community → direct connection** may prove valuable, but that advantage has not been validated yet. We will test it with actual communities before pursuing broad scale.

## Explicit non-goals for the first release

Full parity with Discord, Instagram, WhatsApp or TikTok; traditional group chats; large-scale livestreaming; complex AI ranking; creator marketplaces; federation; and a promise of end-to-end encryption before a reviewed design exists.
